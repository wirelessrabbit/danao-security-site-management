HOW WE OPERATE

1

Site Survey & Consultation

We meet with clients and survey the site where security and protection services are needed.
2

Security Plan

A security plan including post orders will be created to identify the responsibilities and duties of the security guards.
3

Guard Selection

Security guards will be screened and selected through our rigorous screening process to ensure high quality security services.
4

Implementation & Training

The Security Guards will be briefed of post orders and will be provided additional training for specified roles as needed by the client.
5

Supervision

A Supervisor will regularly perform inspections, train new employees, and ensure post orders are followed.

OUR REPORTING SYSTEM

Digital Reporting

All reported issues are time stamped and assigned with automatic tracking numbers for easier recording.

Photo Documentation

Guards are provided with camera equipment to take time-stamped photographs.

Incident Monitoring

Guards receive alerts that they can immediately respond to and report.

Electronic Guard Tour System (EGTS)

Digital tracking system to verify active patrol of guards on assigned checkpoints.

Post Orders

Post Orders are easily accessible to ensure all tasks are completed.

Task Management
